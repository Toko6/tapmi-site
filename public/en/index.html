FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many venues and cards you need, so we tailor it to you. You’ll get a clear offer at the free demo, with no obligation.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Your first 30 days at one venue are free, with no card details needed. If you decide not to continue, keep the cards.

What do I need? Any hardware?

Just the TapMi cards with a QR code — we prepare them for you. Guests use their own phones and you use the dashboard in a browser. Nothing to install.

How long does setup take?

Usually within a week of the demo. We set up your venue and prepare the cards — you just add your staff’s first names.

What does the guest see?

Your logo, five stars and a “Review us on Google” button — always the same. Optionally a private note and Instagram. In Polish, Ukrainian or English.

Can you see how many reviews guests posted?

Your dashboard shows “sent to review sites” — taps on the button. We can’t see whether the guest posted, so we never promise a number of reviews.

Does a manager need an account?

No. Managers get a personal link for alerts by phone and email; the dashboard and settings stay with the owner. There’s also a dark mode for evening service.

What about data and GDPR?

Guests don’t sign up or give a name or email. We store the rating, the optional comment, the time and the card. Staff are added by first name only, and only you see their ratings.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. It’s an open-ended agreement with one month’s notice. You stay because it works, not because you have to.
