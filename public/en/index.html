This is TapMi: the guest page, an alert on your phone and the owner dashboard.

How it works

An NFC card with a QR code: three steps, a few seconds

  1. 1

    The guest taps

    The waiter brings the TapMi card with the bill, or it sits on the table. No NFC? The QR code works too.

  2. 2

    Rates in seconds

    Five stars, plus an optional private note for the team. Then an invitation to review you on Google — the same for everyone.

  3. 3

    You know what’s happening

    Every rating lands in your dashboard. A 1–3★ rating goes straight to your phone and email — or the shift manager’s.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

Rated in seconds

No app, no sign-up. The page speaks Polish, Ukrainian or English to match the guest’s phone, and shows your logo and colours.

Alerts to phone and email

When a guest gives 1–3★, you or your managers hear about it within seconds. Managers don’t even need a login.

A dashboard that shows what matters

It opens with a greeting and whatever needs you. Close an alert with one tap and add a note.

Staff ratings

Each waiter carries their own card — see who guests praise and who might need support. We only compare after 15 ratings.

Comments and stats

Search comments (“wait”, “cold”), ratings per day, star breakdown, export to Excel — and dark mode for evening service.

Instagram button

After rating, guests see “Follow us on Instagram” — an easy way to stay in touch.

For the owner

When something’s off, there’s still time

Every venue has an off moment now and then — a cold plate, a long wait. With TapMi you hear about it right away, not after the fact.

The alert arrives seconds after the rating: how many stars, which venue, who served — with a short tip on how to approach the guest.

The guest leaves feeling heard. You mark it resolved and add a note, e.g. “dessert on the house”.

Every alert reminds you: don’t mention the rating or ask to change it. It’s about the guest, not the stars.

Google-safe

Every guest gets the same invitation

After every rating, from 1 to 5 stars, the guest sees the same “Review us on Google” button. We never pick who gets invited — Google’s policies and EU consumer-review rules don’t allow it — and that keeps your Google reviews safe. The private note for the team is an extra option, never a replacement for a public review.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many venues and cards you need, so we tailor it to you. You’ll get a clear offer at the free demo, with no obligation.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Your first 30 days at one venue are free, with no card details needed. If you decide not to continue, keep the cards.

What do I need? Any hardware?

Just the TapMi cards with a QR code — we prepare them for you. Guests use their own phones and you use the dashboard in a browser. Nothing to install.

How long does setup take?

Usually within a week of the demo. We set up your venue and prepare the cards — you just add your staff’s first names.

What about data and GDPR?

Guests don’t sign up or give a name or email. We store the rating, the optional comment, the time and the card. Staff are added by first name only, and only you see their ratings.

What does the guest see?

Your logo and a question about their visit with five stars. After rating: a thank-you, an option to send the team a private note, a “Review us on Google” button — always the same — and, if you like, Instagram. In Polish, Ukrainian or English.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. It’s an open-ended agreement with one month’s notice. You stay because it works, not because you have to.
